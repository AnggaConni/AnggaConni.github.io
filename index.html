Behavioral Science in Action

Social Proof & Digital Adoption (UNDP)

Accelerated district-level adoption of a digital reporting system in Papua by strategically engaging a respected local officer ("Digital Pioneer"). This sparked peer adoption through social proof, overcoming initial resistance to new technology.

Cognitive Alignment

Boosted compliance with vaccine reporting updates by framing the task not as "admin work" but as "protecting Papua's children," aligning the digital task with health workers' core values.

Loss-Aversion Framing

Strengthened urgency among decision-makers for funding continuity by framing risks as "loss of partner trust" rather than just financial gaps.

Civic Tech & AI Innovation

Heritage Commons (WebGL/Three.js)

Developed a browser-based 3D board game to teach Intangible Cultural Heritage (ICH) safeguarding. Replaced competitive "Monopoly" mechanics with collaborative "Safeguarding" mechanics using AI-generated logic.

AI-Integrated Community Inventory

Engineered a low-code digital ecosystem for a SIDS member nation using Google Stack + Gemini AI. Created a "Single Source of Truth" architecture that prevents database bloat while enabling automated reporting.

IoT for Cold Chain

Integrated IoT sensors with rule-based AI in Papua to monitor vaccine temperatures. The system flagged anomalies automatically, reducing spoilage in remote areas.

Diplomacy & Policy Strategy

Informal Diplomacy (Brunei)

Unlocked stalled cooperation by pivoting from formal meetings to informal relationship building (e.g., casual market visits) with the Director-General of Culture. This "Rapport-Building" approach re-opened communication channels.

Conflict De-escalation (UNESCO)

Mediated a tense multi-country debate on "Rendang" nomination by reframing the issue from "Ownership" to "Shared Media Safeguarding," allowing all parties to save face and move forward.

US Congress Delegation

Managed end-to-end coordination for a high-profile US Congress visit to Jakarta, navigating complex security (UNDSS) and protocol requirements while ensuring cultural heritage goals were met.
